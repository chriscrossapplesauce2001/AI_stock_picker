But the fundamental tape is the opposite of impairment: [FY2025 NBP +12% to $2,782m (42% margin), operating free surplus +15% to $3,059m](https://www.prudentialplc.com/en/newsroom/company-news/2026/prudential-plc-2025-full-year-results/), and [Q1 2026 NBP +10% to $686m with double-digit growth in Hong Kong, Mainland China and Malaysia](https://www.prudentialplc.com/en/newsroom/company-news/2026/prudential-plc-q1-business-performance-update/). ### 2. Tail-Risk Stress Test
